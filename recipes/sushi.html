Sushi

Description

a traditional Japanese dish made with vinegared rice combined with other items like seafood, vegetables, or egg. Many people think sushi means raw fish, but the word actually refers to the sour-tasted seasoned rice itself. Raw fish is common, but sushi can also use cooked ingredients or no fish at all.

The most interesting fact about sushi is that it was originally used as cash to pay taxes in 8th-century Japan.At that time, sushi was not a fresh dish, but a method of preserving fish by packing it in fermented rice. Because it kept for a long time and was highly valuable, people could use it as a recognized currency to pay their annual government taxes

Ingredients

  • 6 sheets sushi seaweed aka nori
  • 1 batch prepared sushi rice
  • 1/2 lb sashimi-grade raw salmon or desired raw fish of choice
  • 4 oz cream cheese sliced into strips
  • 1 avocado sliced
  • soy sauce for serving

Steps

  1. Place the seaweed on a bamboo mat, then cover the sheet of seaweed with an even layer of prepared sushi rice. Smooth gently with a rice paddle.
  2. Layer salmon, cream cheese, and avocado on the rice, and roll it up tightly. Slice with a sharp knife, and enjoy right away with soy sauce.
